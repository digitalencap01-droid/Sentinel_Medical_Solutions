# Sentinel website image policy

Sentinel will not use AI-generated photography or AI-generated people anywhere
on this website.

All website photography must be one of the following:

- Authentic photography supplied and approved by Sentinel.
- Properly licensed stock photography from a reputable image library.
- Leadership portraits supplied or formally approved by the individual shown.
